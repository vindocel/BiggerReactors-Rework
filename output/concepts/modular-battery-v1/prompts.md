# Prompts — bateria modular, conceito 01

Gerado com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. Referências visuais: texturas locais do reator, turbina, vidro e ultimate_induction_provider do Mekanism fornecidas pelo usuário. Estes arquivos são pranchas de conceito, não atlas UV ou sprites finais.

## Prancha de componentes

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Minecraft mod texture concept sheet, landscape, high clarity.
Create ONE coherent concept board for a new modular battery in BiggerReactors-Rework, titled "BATERIA MODULAR — CONCEITO 01". This is an art-direction proposal, not final game-ready UV textures.
Input images are STYLE REFERENCES only: reactor corner/frame/face/disassembled are tiny 16x16 industrial pixel textures; glass is connected glass reference; induction provider is design context only, DO NOT copy its magenta-green palette or motifs.
Match the restrained original Minecraft 16x16 pixel art vocabulary, big readable pixels, simple recessed metal plates, small rivets and 1-pixel bevels, limited palette, nearest-neighbor enlarged. Do NOT create ultra-detailed sci-fi or smooth photoreal surfaces. Each flat swatch should visually be a credible 16x16 texture enlarged cleanly.
New palette: muted warm pewter/graphite casing #555853 #74766B #96988B, dark seams #272C2B, subtle copper #A97848, pale amber energy #E9BB62. Copper used as small accent, not gold armor. Same art quality and industrial design language as BiggerReactors, distinct from dark-gray reactor and light blue-gray turbine. No purple, neon green or cyan as dominant colors.
Layout: charcoal background, clean Portuguese labels, generous spacing. Top left a row of four separate square front-facing swatches labeled "Desmontada", "Canto", "Aresta", "Face". Aresta has uninterrupted parallel rails that can run through adjacent tiles, canto terminates perpendicular rails with four small bolts, face is restrained almost plain plate. Show a small 3-tile joined rail sample underneath to establish connected casing logic.
Middle left: glass standalone swatch and connected 2x2 window sample labeled "Vidro isolado" and "Vidro conectado". Thin warm-metal perimeter border, nearly clear subtly smoke-tinted center, sparse single pixel reflections, remove shared internal borders in joined glass. The connected sample is 2x2 tiles only for demonstrating adjacency, not the 3x3x3 machine.
Bottom left: distinct energy input/output front swatches labeled "Entrada" and "Saída", identical recessed square sockets and amber/copper identity, with inward vs outward arrow silhouettes so direction reads without relying on color. Next a row of 3 terminal front swatches labeled "Desligado", "Espera", "Ativo": dark screen; dim amber screen; bright amber charge bars with tiny ivory status pixels. Keep terminal industrial and square, not computer display high resolution.
RIGHT THIRD: large isolated isometric ONE-BLOCK energy cell design, label "Célula de energia", plus small orthographic front and top texture suggestions underneath. A cube-sized metal cage, four stout corner supports, recessed stack of three rectangular capacitor plates, copper busbars and modest amber charge slits. All geometry made only of axis-aligned Minecraft cuboids, no cylinders, no floating magic, no extruding cables. This one cell combines storage AND transfer, no separate provider. Caption "Armazenamento + transferência". This is the same cell to be used inside a future 3x3x3 battery. Flat swatches and isometric block must share palette and motifs.
Footer small label "ESTUDO VISUAL · TEXTURAS A FINALIZAR EM 16×16". Clean accurate labels, no unrelated branding, no code.

## Correção visual aplicada

Edit this concept board precisely, preserving ALL layout, palette, casing swatches, energy cell geometry, labels and pixel-art style. Correct ONLY TWO issues:
1. The sample labeled "Vidro conectado (2 × 2)" must become a single seamless large transparent smoky window framed ONLY along the outside perimeter. Preserve outer copper/metal border. Tiny faint tick marks outside the frame may indicate 2x2 tile divisions, but the pane itself is continuous.
2. In the bottom row change the arrow inside the socket labeled "Entrada" to point DOWNWARD into the socket, and change the arrow inside the socket labeled "Saída" to point UPWARD out of the socket. Thus the two arrow silhouettes are clearly OPPOSITE. Preserve their amber color, all socket housing geometry, all other elements exactly.

## Prancha de montagem

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a second landscape Minecraft concept board titled "BATERIA MODULAR — MONTAGEM 3 × 3 × 3". Use the attached finished concept board as the exact design reference for warm pewter/graphite metal, subtle copper rails, amber status bars, terminal, ports and internal capacitor cell. Keep blocky 16x16-style pixel textures, same industrial BiggerReactors appearance, nearest-neighbor crisp pixels. This is a modest Minecraft block construction, NOT a futuristic high-detail large machine.
CRITICAL GEOMETRY: external volume EXACTLY THREE unit blocks wide, THREE high, THREE deep. Wall thickness ONE full block. Therefore interior is EXACTLY ONE unit cube at center. Every external face has a 3x3 arrangement: corner-edge-corner / edge-CENTER-edge / corner-edge-corner. The only replaceable face tile is the SINGLE CENTER tile. Absolutely no 2x2 windows, no 4x4 block faces, no 3x2 windows. Visible single center window MUST occupy exactly one ninth of the face area. All 20 perimeter blocks form connected solid casing: corners and straight rails. Show small dim subdivisions between structural blocks where necessary for countability; copper rails run continuously across matching casing edges. Cube sides visually count 3, not 2 or 4.
Layout THREE panels on dark charcoal backdrop:
LEFT largest panel "Montada": large isometric assembled 3x3x3 cube viewed from front-right-above. Front face center is the single 1x1 amber terminal referenced, right face center is a single 1x1 smoked-glass window showing the central cell through it, top face center is a single 1x1 glass inspection window. Remaining tiles are thick casing with reference corners and rails. No other windows or ports in perimeter. Tiny axis dimension braces read "3 blocos" on width,height,depth.
MIDDLE panel "Vista traseira": smaller isometric same exact cube viewed from back-left-above. Back face single CENTER tile is the reference input socket with a DOWN arrow; left face single CENTER tile is output with UP arrow. Top single center glass window. Casing wraps continuously, same design. Clearly label "Entrada" pointing to back center and "Saída" pointing to left center. Ports do not protrude beyond full cube and no pipes/cables.
RIGHT panel "Corte de estudo": isometric sectional view of the SAME 3x3x3 cube, with front and right shell surfaces omitted strictly for illustration, exposing the ONE 1x1x1 central energy cell. Draw faint thin dashed external 3x3x3 bounding box where walls removed. Remaining back wall and bottom floor show thickness one full block. Central cell sits at center coordinates (1,1,1), above one-block-thick bottom casing, and occupies only 1/27 total volume. It is NOT a huge cell filling all 3 blocks. Cell matches reference: four stout pewter posts, three stacked capacitor plates with amber slits, copper vertical busbars, square top cap, voxel cuboid geometry. Label "1 célula interna" and note "Corte ilustrativo".
Footer: "3×3×3 externo · 1×1×1 interno · Célula única: armazenamento + transferência".
No giant panels or misplaced terminal on edge/corner. No multiple cells, no open vents mistaken for windows, no invented numerical energy specs. All parts look like the reference concept. Pixelated restrained art, clean accurate Portuguese typography.
